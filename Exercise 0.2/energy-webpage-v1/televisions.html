Televisions and Energy

Screen technology, screen size and viewing time can affect how much electricity a television uses.

Energy labels

An energy label provides an estimate of yearly electricity use. It is most useful when comparing televisions of a similar size.

Common questions

Does a larger screen always use more energy?

Larger screens often need more energy, but screen technology and efficiency also matter.

What does kWh mean?

A kilowatt-hour is a unit used to measure electrical energy.
